LEARN TO TOP ROPE

Step 2: Tie In

For safety, when top roping, you must tie into the pulley system when climbing

STEP 1

Begin by pulling the end of the rope through both loops of your harness. *It is very important to do both loops. If you only do one and you fall, you may end up upside down like Spiderman.

STEP 2

Begin feedin the rope through the bottom loop of the figure 8 knot*, following it the whole way around the top (think railroad tracks). *The figure 8 knot should be tied for you already. If not, see separate tutorial on a figure 8 knot.

STEP 3

Pull the end of the rope through the bottom ono the figure 8 knot, continuing to follow the rope all the way through.

STEP 4

Pull the end of the rope around the bottom loop, and back up through the top of the knot. The end of the rope should be parallel to the rope leading up to the top of the wall.

STEP 5

Pull the end of the rope around the bottom loop, and back up through the top of the knot. The end of the rope should be parallel to the rope leading up to the top of the wall.

STEP 6

Pull the top knot tight, and you are finished! You are now ready to start your climb. Make sure your belayer is all set, and then move on to the next step!
