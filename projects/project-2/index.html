HOW TO ROCK CLIMB IN AN INDOOR GYM

Choose a style of climb:

Bouldering

Free climbing without a rope, on shorter walls, with thick crash mats below to cushion falls.

Learn to Boulder

Top Rope

A style of rock climbing where a rope connects you to a fixed anchor at the top of the route, then runs down to your partner (the belayer) on the ground, allowing you to climb higher and safer.

Learn to Top Rope
