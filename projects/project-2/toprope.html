LEARN TO TOP ROPE

1/6

Step 1: Equipment

You will need these items:

HARNESS

A harness is used to help tie you into the pulley system which allows you to safely climb. Using a harness provides critical support and safety to the climber.

CARABINER

Not necessary to the climber, but critical for the person belaying (or helping secure the climber and ensure their safety). The Carabiner is what the rope is fed through to create a strong and reliable connection between the person belaying, the rope, and the climber.

ROPE

Necessary to both the climber and the one belaying, the rope provides safety when top roping in an indoor (and even outdoor) areas. The rope will help hold the climber as they go up the wall.

CLIMBING SHOES

More tightly fitted to your feet than sneakers, climbing shoes help give you more support nd friction for a more precise hold on the wall.

CHALK

Not required, but highly recommended. Using chalk helps absorb sweat and oils from your hands, allowing for better grip on holds.

A FRIEND

Since top roping involves climbing much higher than bouldering, you will need a friend or buddy to tie in with you to help propel you up the wall and keep you safe from a dangerous fall.
