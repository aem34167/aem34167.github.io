Step 2: Choose Difficulty

Each number represents a difficulty level - The higher the number, the harder the climb.

BEGINNER

V0-V1

These climbs are heavily geared towards new climbers. They often have deep pockets and excellent grips, making for easy climbs. These routes are good for learning the basics.

INTERMEDIATE

V2-V5

These routes are more designed for people who are a bit more experienced climbers, with smaller holds and trickier moves. These are great for building strength after learning the basics.

HARD

V6+

These routes are geared towards seasoned climbers with loads of experience. They feature the trickiest moves and difficult holds.
