Step 3: Grab the Start

There are two different kinds of start holds, the tag will specify which one it is. The tag is usually the beginning of the route.

NORMAL START

With a regular start, all you have to do is put both of your hands on the same hold (typically the one with the tag, signifying the difficulty), and you are all ready to go!

TWO HAND START

With a two hand start, it becomes a little bit more complicated. One hand will start on the hold that is with the tag, and the other will start on the hold designated by the route setter (usually marked with a triangle). Once you have done that, you are all ready to go!
