Step 1: Equipment

You will need these items:

CLIMBING SHOES

More tightly fitted to your feet than sneakers, climbing shoes help give you more support nd friction for a more precise hold on the wall.

CHALK

Not required, but highly recommended. Using chalk helps absorb sweat and oils from your hands, allowing for better grip on holds.

YOURSELF!

All you need is your body and a can-do attitude and you are all set to start your climb!
